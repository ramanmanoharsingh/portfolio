How Ratings Work

Transparent by design — every player can see exactly how their number is calculated.

1180
Rating
new_rating = old_rating + K × (actual − expected)  ± peer_feedback_adj
1Starting point. Self-declared skill sets a base band — Beginner starts at 900, Intermediate at 1100, Advanced at 1300 — so no one starts from zero credibility.
2Match outcomes. After every logged match, both players' ratings move using a standard Elo update (K=32) — beating a higher-rated player earns more than beating a lower-rated one.
3Peer feedback. Opponents rate sportsmanship and reliability (1–5 stars) after each match. The average nudges rating by at most ±20 points — enough to matter, not enough to be gamed by outcomes alone.
4Why it's fair. No single win, loss, or five-star review can swing a rating dramatically — it takes a consistent pattern, the same way real skill shows up over many games, not one lucky match.
